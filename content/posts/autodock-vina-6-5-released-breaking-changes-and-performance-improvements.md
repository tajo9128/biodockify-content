<p>AutoDock Vina, the most widely used open-source molecular docking program, has released version 6.5 with significant breaking changes and substantial performance improvements.</p>
<p>This release brings enhanced scoring functions, better handling of flexible residues, and improved parallel execution capabilities.</p>
