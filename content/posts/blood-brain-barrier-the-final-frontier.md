<p>The blood-brain barrier (BBB) represents one of the greatest challenges in neurological drug development. This dynamic interface regulates molecular transport between blood and brain, preventing 98% of potential therapeutics from reaching their targets.</p>
<p>Recent advances in BBB research are generating excitement. Scientists at University of Pennsylvania and elsewhere are developing receptor-mediated transcytosis vectors capable of transporting large molecules across the barrier. Focused ultrasound technology temporarily opens the BBB for drug delivery.</p>
<p>Nature Reviews Drug Discovery highlighted BBB dysfunction as an underappreciated therapeutic target in aging and diseases including Alzheimer, Parkinson, and multiple sclerosis.</p>
<p>Computational approaches are crucial here. Molecular dynamics simulations can model drug-BBB interactions, while machine learning predicts blood-brain penetration.</p>
<p>The global BBB therapeutics market is projected to reach $15 billion by 2030.</p>
