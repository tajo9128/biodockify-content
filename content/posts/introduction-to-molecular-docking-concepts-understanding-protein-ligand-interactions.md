<p>Molecular docking is a computational technique that predicts the preferred orientation of a small molecule (ligand) when bound to a larger receptor (protein).</p>
<p>This comprehensive guide explores the fundamental concepts, mathematical models, and computational methods that make molecular docking possible.</p>
