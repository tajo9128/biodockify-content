<p>Fetal brain organoids are revolutionizing understanding of neurodevelopment and neurological disease. These 3D brain tissue cultures derived from stem cells recapitulate aspects of human brain development.</p>
<p>Researchers use organoids to study how Zika virus impacts neurodevelopment, revealing mechanisms of congenital Zika syndrome. Similar approaches explore autism spectrum disorders, schizophrenia, and intellectual disabilities.</p>
<p>Organoids enable studies impossible in humans—examining fetal brain development, testing teratogenic effects of drugs, and modeling genetic disorders. They bridge the gap between animal models and human physiology.</p>
<p>Challenges remain: organoids lack full vascularization and immune components. Maturation to adult-like states proves difficult. Reproducibility varies between laboratories.</p>
