<p>The pharmaceutical industry R&#038;D engine remains productive despite challenges. Evaluate Omnium projects 57 novel agents launching in the US during 2026, generating projected fifth-year sales of nearly $50 billion.</p>
<p>This aligns with the decade average, indicating stable productivity. However, the landscape is evolving. Specialty drugs, biologics, and gene therapies increasingly dominate approvals.</p>
<p>Oncology continues leading therapeutic areas, followed by immunology, neurology, and rare diseases. Cell and gene therapies represent the fastest-growing segment.</p>
<p>FDA leadership turnover and staffing cuts have raised concerns about consistency. Sponsors report increased difficulty obtaining early development guidance.</p>
<p>The 2026 landscape includes several anticipated blockbusters: next-generation GLP-1 agonists, novel immunotherapies, and first-in-class mechanisms for genetic diseases.</p>
