<p>Rare diseases and complex neurological disorders represent major focus areas for pharmaceutical R&#038;D. Conditions like ALS, Alzheimer, and Parkinson affect millions but have historically lacked effective treatments.</p>
<p>Gene therapy advances are transforming rare disease treatment. SMA patients treated with Zolgensma show dramatic improvements. Lysosomal storage disorders, hemophilia, and immunodeficiencies benefit from gene therapy approaches.</p>
<p>In neurology, the focus is shifting to earlier intervention and precision medicine. Alzheimer research emphasizes biomarker-driven prevention trials. ALS research explores genetic targets and neuroprotection.</p>
<p>The economic model for rare diseases has matured. Orphan drug designations provide regulatory incentives, and payer frameworks increasingly recognize the value of transformative therapies.</p>
