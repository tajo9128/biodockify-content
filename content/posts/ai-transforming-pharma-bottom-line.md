<p>Artificial intelligence is revolutionizing pharmaceutical R&#038;D, but not always in the ways initially predicted. While splashy announcements about AI-discovered drugs grab headlines, the real transformation is happening in operational efficiency and decision-making.</p>
<p>Pharma companies are deploying AI across the value chain: target identification, lead optimization, clinical trial design, patient recruitment, and manufacturing. Bristol Myers Squibb partnership with Exscientia and Novartis collaboration with DeepMind exemplify this trend.</p>
<p>The economic impact is substantial. AI-powered target validation can reduce research timelines by 18-24 months. Predictive analytics in clinical trials cut costs by 15-20% through better site selection and patient matching.</p>
<p>However, challenges persist. Data quality and integration remain significant hurdles. Regulatory frameworks are still evolving to address AI-generated findings.</p>
<p>For molecular docking specialists, AI presents opportunities in virtual screening acceleration, binding affinity prediction, and ADMET modeling.</p>
