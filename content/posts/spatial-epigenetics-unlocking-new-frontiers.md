<p>Spatial epigenetics represents an emerging frontier combining spatial genomics with epigenetic profiling, revealing how gene regulation varies across tissue microenvironments.</p>
<p>Traditional epigenomics loses spatial context. New technologies like 10x Genomics Visium and NanoString CosMX preserve spatial information while profiling DNA methylation, histone modifications, and chromatin accessibility.</p>
<p>This approach is transforming cancer research. Tumors are not uniform; they contain diverse microenvironments with distinct epigenetic landscapes.</p>
<p>Drug resistance mechanisms are becoming clearer through spatial analysis. Researchers can now identify epigenetic safe havens where cancer cells escape therapy.</p>
<p>For pharmaceutical companies, spatial epigenetics enables better biomarker discovery, patient stratification, and combination therapy design.</p>
