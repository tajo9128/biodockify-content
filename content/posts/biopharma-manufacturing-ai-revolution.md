<p>Biopharmaceutical manufacturing is undergoing digital transformation through AI and advanced analytics. This revolution addresses quality control, supply chain efficiency, and process optimization.</p>
<p>AI-powered process analytical technology (PAT) enables real-time monitoring of critical quality attributes. Machine learning models predict product quality from process parameters, reducing batch failures and improving yield.</p>
<p>Supply chain optimization benefits from demand forecasting, inventory management, and logistics AI. The complexity of biologics supply chains makes AI indispensable.</p>
<p>Regulatory agencies encourage advanced manufacturing. The FDA Emerging Technology Program facilitates adoption of continuous manufacturing, real-time release, and digital twins.</p>
